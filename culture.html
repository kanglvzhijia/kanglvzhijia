Our Core Values

These principles form the foundation of everything we do, guiding our decisions and shaping our relationships with customers, partners, and each other.

01

Innovation

We continuously push boundaries, embracing new technologies and methodologies to deliver groundbreaking solutions in health and wellness. Innovation is the engine that drives our growth and differentiation.

02

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in everything we do, from the quality of our services to the precision of our technology. Excellence is not just a goal—it's our standard of operation.

03

Wellness

The health and wellbeing of our customers and team members is at the heart of our mission. We are committed to promoting healthy lifestyles and creating positive impacts on individual and community wellness.

04

Integrity

We conduct business with honesty, transparency, and ethical practices. Our commitment to integrity builds trust with our stakeholders and ensures sustainable, long-term relationships.

05

Collaboration

We believe in the power of teamwork and partnerships. By working together—internally and with our global partners—we achieve greater outcomes and create shared value for all stakeholders.

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable business practices that respect the environment and contribute to long-term societal wellbeing. Our decisions consider the impact on future generations.

Our Journey

Milestones of Excellence

From our founding to today, each milestone represents our commitment to growth, innovation, and delivering exceptional wellness solutions.

2019

Company Founded

Beijing Xingyun Yidao Cultural Communication Co., Ltd. established with a vision to transform health and wellness services through technology and innovation.

2020

Health Tourism Launch

Successfully launched our health tourism services, combining travel with wellness monitoring and therapeutic experiences across premium destinations.

2021

Mobile App Development

Released our first mobile applications on Google Play and App Store, bringing health tracking and wellness management to users' fingertips.

2022

Medical Device Expansion

Expanded into medical devices, offering Class I and Class II products with quality certifications and regulatory compliance for global markets.

2023

Event & Hotel Services

Launched professional event planning and hotel management services, providing comprehensive solutions for exhibitions, conferences, and corporate events.

2024

Global Expansion

Continuing to grow with enhanced mobile apps, expanded wellness services, and a stronger global presence serving customers across multiple continents.
