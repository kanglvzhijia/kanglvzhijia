FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services, mobile apps, and how to get the most out of KangLv ZhiJia.

How can I download your mobile applications?

You can download our mobile applications from both Google Play Store (for Android devices) and Apple App Store (for iOS devices). Simply search for "KangLv ZhiJia" or click on the app store badges on our website.

What services do you offer for health tourism?

We offer comprehensive health tourism services including travel arrangements, accommodation booking, health consultation, wellness programs, and therapeutic experiences. Our services are designed to combine health monitoring with relaxing travel experiences.

How do I contact customer support for app issues?

For any issues with our mobile applications, please email us at support@kanglvzhijia.com with details about the problem you're experiencing. Our technical support team will assist you within 24 hours.

Do you offer services outside of China?

Yes, we serve customers globally. Our mobile applications are available worldwide, and we partner with wellness facilities across multiple countries. Please contact us to discuss your specific needs.

What types of medical devices do you offer?

We offer a range of medical devices including Class I (ready-to-ship) and Class II (filing required) products. These include diagnostic equipment, health monitoring devices, and wellness-related medical instruments.

How can I become a business partner?

We welcome partnership inquiries from businesses in the health, wellness, tourism, and technology sectors. Please reach out to guolin@kanglvzhijia.com with your partnership proposal and company information.
